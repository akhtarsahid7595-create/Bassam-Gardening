How We Work

Our Design Process

A refined, client-centred process that transforms your vision into a living outdoor masterpiece.

01

Consultation

We meet at your site, listen to your aspirations, assess the space, and discuss your lifestyle needs, budget, and timeline. No obligation.

02

Site Planning

Our designers develop a detailed landscape plan — considering sunlight, drainage, soil conditions, and the unique microclimates of your location in Dar es Salaam.

03

Design & Build

Our skilled installation teams bring the plan to life — every plant positioned with intent, every stone laid with care, every pathway finished to perfection.

04

Finishing & Care

Final quality review, client walkthrough, and optional ongoing maintenance programme — ensuring your landscape remains stunning for years ahead.
